# Icon and brand asset notes

This project combines several asset categories:

## Rizvisions

- The Rizvisions eye and wordmark were supplied by Rizvisions and adapted into a macOS-style application canvas for this site.

## Apple-style system apps

- Finder, Messages, Photos, Settings, Terminal, folder, and Trash use current-style macOS artwork prepared for this interface.
- Some source references were obtained from publicly available icon-history and Wikimedia pages; others are clean site-ready reconstructions matching the current visual language.
- They are used here only to simulate a personal macOS desktop. Apple and macOS are trademarks of Apple Inc.; this site is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.
- `EXTRACT_MACOS_SYSTEM_ICONS.command` can replace supported files with the exact application resources installed on the site owner's Mac.

## Third-party apps and brands

- Instagram and Spotify marks remain trademarks of their respective owners.
- Parker artwork is a custom site icon based on the Parker brand identity.

## Custom site icons

- Safari, QuickTime Player, Mail, document, Parker, and Rizvisions variants were prepared specifically for this site where a directly reusable exact asset was unavailable or inappropriate.

Do not redistribute this icon folder as a standalone icon pack. It is bundled as part of the Rizvisions personal-site interface.
