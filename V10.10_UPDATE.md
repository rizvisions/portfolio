# V10.10 update

This pass removes the floating Currently widget and replaces the handwritten hello with a brief liquid-glass focus transition. The time-aware greeting remains available during the intro and in Notification Center.

Terminal now presents a white Rizvisions wordmark and responds to a deliberately small set of natural-language questions without pretending to be a real LLM. Messages now has two usable threads, a real text field, and clear LinkedIn and Instagram actions. The Spotify embed fills the full window canvas.

The update also corrects the desktop coordinate used to center media above the Dock and avoids re-centering a video after metadata loads when its window already matches the source aspect ratio.
